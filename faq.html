Frequently asked questions

Who can join?

Mixed-gender inclusive football from age 7 to adult.

Do I need experience?

No. New players are welcome.

Where and when is training?

V36 synthetic pitch, Wednesdays: ages 7–16, 7–8pm; seniors 16+, 8–9pm.

Is one-to-one coaching available?

The club does not provide one-to-one coaching during group training.

How do I join?

Contact the club before attending your first session. Join us.

How do I install the app?

Open the club app in Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone and use the browser's Add to Home Screen option.

How do I access private information?

Use your approved account in the Club Hub. Hub access is currently restricted to approved adults aged 18 or over.
