Frequently asked questions

Do I need football experience?

No. We welcome beginners and experienced players.

Is the club mixed gender?

Yes. We welcome boys, girls, men and women.

Do you provide one-to-one coaching?

Our training is group-based. We do not provide dedicated one-to-one coaching. Please discuss any support needs privately with the club before attending.

What should I bring?

Suitable sports clothing, footwear for a synthetic pitch and water. Ask a coach about any additional equipment before your first session.

How much does it cost?

Contact the club for current membership fees and arrangements.

How do I become a coach or volunteer?

Send an enquiry above. The club will discuss suitable roles, safeguarding and any required checks.
