Disability football explained

Inclusive football gives people of different abilities opportunities to enjoy the game, build confidence and make friends. No previous football experience is needed.

Your first session

Contact the club before attending. Bring suitable footwear, comfortable sports clothing and water. Let coaches know about any support needs before training.

Wednesday at V36: ages 7–16, 7–8pm; seniors 16+, 8–9pm. Check the calendar for breaks.
