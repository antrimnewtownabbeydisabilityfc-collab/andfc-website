Safeguarding, Welfare & Support

Everyone deserves to feel safe, respected and included.

Raise a concern

Immediate danger

If someone is in immediate danger or needs emergency medical assistance, call 999. Do not wait for a club response.

Club safeguarding and welfare

Speak privately to the club safeguarding or welfare officer, or ask the club to arrange a private conversation. The general club email is not a dedicated confidential reporting inbox. Do not include sensitive personal information in an initial email.

Request private contact

If the concern involves a club official, or you feel unable to report internally, contact the Irish FA safeguarding team, the relevant Health and Social Care Trust Gateway service or police directly. You do not need club permission to report a concern.

Listen, take concerns seriously, avoid investigating yourself and share information only with those who need it to protect someone. Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised where safety is at risk.

Respect and inclusion

Code of conduct

Treat players, officials, coaches and families with respect. Bullying, racism, sectarianism, sexism, harassment and discrimination have no place in football.

Equality and inclusion

Respect different abilities, backgrounds and support needs. Encourage participation, dignity and teamwork.

Online safety

Do not share private information, abusive messages or photographs without appropriate permission. Report bullying or inappropriate contact privately. Club Hub is not continuously monitored and must not be used for urgent safeguarding reports.

These are summary expectations, not the full formal club policies. Contact the club for current approved copies.

Parents, guardians and coaches

Parents and guardians

Arrive 15 minutes before training, communicate absences and discuss support needs privately with coaches. Training is group-based; dedicated one-to-one coaching is not provided.

Coaches and volunteers

Follow club safeguarding procedures, maintain appropriate boundaries, report concerns promptly and complete required safeguarding training and AccessNI checks where applicable.
