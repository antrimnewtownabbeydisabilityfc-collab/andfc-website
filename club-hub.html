Keep our hub welcoming

Friendly banter should make everyone feel included. No bullying, discriminatory remarks, sexual content, threats or jokes targeting another member. Do not share personal information, private messages or safeguarding concerns in posts.

Every post and reply needs coach approval. Reports go to coaches and hide the reported post until it is reviewed. The hub is not monitored continuously.

If something worries you, speak privately to Ryan Hamilton, our club welfare/safeguarding officer, or another coach. If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.

Junior access is through a parent or guardian account approved by the club. Junior and adult discussions are separate; authorised coaches can moderate both.
