How it all began

In 2020, during the COVID-19 pandemic, Jim McCracken founded Antrim Newtownabbey Disability FC after parents encouraged him to return to disability football. The club began with a small senior squad and a simple ambition: give people of different abilities a place to play football, make friends and feel they belong.

Restrictions made regular training difficult, but players, parents and volunteers persevered. As football returned, the club developed its junior provision in 2021, attracting significant interest from local families. Parents and volunteers became part of the coaching community, helping the club grow.

Our motto, Ability Not Disability, reflects the belief that everyone deserves an opportunity to take part.

Our journey

2020

The club is founded during the pandemic.

2021

Junior football expands opportunities for younger players.

2021–2022

The Irish FA recognises AND FC among clubs providing disability football opportunities.

March 2023

Charity registration achieved: NIC109278.

May 2023

The club records its first league trophy, with the second senior team finishing runners-up in its division, as reported in the NACN newsletter.

November 2023

The club joins the Special Olympics programme. Founder Jim McCracken is recognised with the national Irish FA Football for All award in 2023.

2024

Regional Irish FA Football for All award recognition, reported by the Newtownabbey Times.

2025

The club is mentioned in the Northern Ireland Assembly during a discussion of sports funding and disability inclusion.

2026

The club develops its women's senior provision and fields Eagles, Hawks and Cubs in inclusion football. Community wellbeing, respect and inclusion remain central to club activities.

2027 — looking ahead

Plans include a trip to Stirling, subject to arrangements and fundraising.

More than football

AND FC works to create a welcoming environment where people can build confidence, stay active and develop friendships. The club brings together families, coaches, volunteers and community partners from across the borough and beyond. Its work extends to wellbeing, respect, inclusion and community participation.

Every achievement belongs to the players and the people who support them. Our story continues with each training session, match and community event.
