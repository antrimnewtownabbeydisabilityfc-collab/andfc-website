Keep our hub welcoming

Friendly banter should make everyone feel included. No bullying, discriminatory remarks, sexual content, threats or jokes targeting another member. Do not share personal information, private messages or safeguarding concerns in posts.

Every post and reply needs coach approval. Reports go to coaches and hide the reported post until it is reviewed. The hub is not monitored continuously.

If something worries you, speak privately to Ryan Hamilton, our club welfare/safeguarding officer, or another coach. If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.

Access is restricted to approved adult members aged 18 and over.

Fundraising management

Administrator only. Record actual receipts and expenses separately from pledges. Permanent entries; do not enter the same receipt twice. Top Bins: £2 per three shots, four weeks, 50% gross prize. Use consistent player names.

Club financial management

Administrator only. Manual cash ledger, not a bank feed or statutory accounts. Enter opening balances once per fund; do not duplicate fees or savings. Use consistent fund names. Entries are permanent; correct errors with a clearly described opposite entry.

Coach and volunteer management

Administrator only. Record confirmation status and club review dates, not certificate numbers, criminal history or documents. A confirmed status is not a substitute for safeguarding checks. Do not enter real records until permissions have been tested.
